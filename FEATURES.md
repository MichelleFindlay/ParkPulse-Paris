# Park Pulse: features (v{{version}})

Park Pulse is a private web app for our Disneyland Paris trip (Disneyland Park and Disney Adventure World, {{trip_dates}}). It covers live waits, food, maps and navigation, planning, notifications and a full guide.

## ⏱ Waits tab
- **Live waits for both parks:** standby, single rider, and Premier Access price and return time. Colour-coded and updated every minute.
- **Fallback:** Queue-Times takes over if the main live feed (ThemeParks.wiki) goes down.
- **Ride hints:**
  - **Trend arrows** compare now with 30–60 minutes ago.
  - **"Usually X"** shows what's typical for this hour on earlier days.
  - **Wait predictions** cover the next few hours.
- **Downtime:** "down since 14:05 (25 min)", "back up 5 min ago", and breakdowns today.
- **Crowd meter:** a 1–5 level with today's curve.
- **🔮 Later view:** predicted waits for the next 4 hours on every ride.
- **☔ Rain plan:** indoor and covered rides only (also suggested when rain is forecast).
- **Single-rider and Premier Access hints:** "single rider saves 35 min", "Premier Access likely gone ~15:30".
- **Premier Access verdict** on each ride: worth it, maybe, use single rider, or wait instead.
- **Filters and sorting:** open / ★ / all; sort by wait, land or A–Z; search with the 🔍 button.
- **On each ride card:** a sparkline of today's waits, ride facts (indoor, minimum height, intensity, length), ★ must-do, 🔔 alerts, 📍 map, and "I rode it" logging.
- **⏱ Queue timer:** tap "I'm in the queue", then "I'm on" when you board. Your real wait goes in the ride log next to what the sign said.
- **Next up** card from your day plan, plus a rope-drop banner before opening.

## ✨ Shows tab
- **Today's shows and character meets,** with the next time and a countdown. Attractions with show times (e.g. Mickey's PhilharMagic) are listed here too.
- **★ a show** to get a reminder about 15 minutes before it starts.
- **🚶 Leave alert:** works out your walk and tells you when to set off (25 minutes early for parades and night shows).
- **Toggles:** reserved viewing areas, and upcoming vs all of today.
- **Only on days they run:** shows appear on the days they're scheduled, and a **Completed shows** list at the bottom holds the ones that have finished.
- **🆕 New:** a daily check tags new rides, shows and restaurants for 14 days and lists them on Trip.

## 🍴 Eat & shop tab
- **Park restaurants** with live status, today's hours (live or from Disney's schedule) and trip-day hours.
- **Disney Village and the Disney hotels'** restaurants and bars.
- **Hours from several sources,** each clearly labelled: OpenStreetMap, web research (with source and date) or your own entries.
- **📄 Official menu PDFs** for 42 of 52 park restaurants, with a fallback menu directory.
- **⭐ Party ratings** and notes.
- **🥗 Diet badges and filter:** vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, lactose-free, halal and kosher, from OpenStreetMap. Coverage is partial (21 restaurants tagged), so always check allergens with staff.
- **🛍 Shops** in each park and Disney Village (from OpenStreetMap), grouped by land with "near X" hints.
- **Filters, sorting and search,** with ★ and a map link on every card.

## 🗺 Map tab
- **A built-in map** with your GPS position, colour-coded ride waits, and markers for shows, food and shops.
- **🚻 Essentials:** toilets (with baby-changing and wheelchair flags), water fountains, first aid, defibrillators, cash machines and lockers. **🚻 Nearest** takes you to the closest toilet.
- **More layers,** each with its own toggle: ☂️ rain shelters and covered walkways, 🪑 benches and picnic tables, 🚬 smoking areas, 📸 viewpoints.
- **Near you:** everything within 700 m, nearest first.
- **🧭 Live navigation:**
  - **Turn-by-turn walking routes** on the parks' footpaths, with backstage paths excluded.
  - **Re-routing** when you go off route, and an arrival alert.
  - **Optional voice** for real turns; the screen stays awake.
  - **♿ Step-free mode** avoids steps.
- **Handoffs to Google Maps / Apple Maps.**
- **👥 Party map:** opt-in location sharing, a 📌 meet-up pin, and "Pin me".
- **/// what3words** for any place or your own position (needs a paid key).
- **⬇ Offline map:** map tiles you've viewed are kept.

## 🗓 Trip tab
- **Countdown** to the trip.
- **🎟 Park tickets:** scan everyone's tickets with the camera or from a photo or screenshot; the app reads each QR code with its own built-in reader and redraws an exact, crisp copy, then shows them full screen at the gates (screen kept awake, flip between people). Works offline; tickets stay on the phone and are never uploaded.
- **📦 Trip pack:** downloads everything for use with no signal, kept permanently and refreshed automatically.
- **📆 Crowd calendar:** predicted level 1–10 per park per day, with park hours, Extra Magic Hours, events and the reasons. Shows the measured level once there's data, and lets you pin dates.
- **🧭 Plan my day:**
  - **Ready-made plans** for small kids, older kids, adults, seniors or mixed groups, or build your own.
  - **Routes rides by quiet times and walking,** around shows and bookings.
  - **Re-optimises itself** through the day.
- **📅 Bookings:** restaurants, Premier Access returns and more, shared with the party, with reminders.
- **🌅 Rope drop:** first rides in order, Extra Magic Hours rides, tips, and the queues that build fastest after opening (from our own data).
- **🚆 RER A trains:** live departures and disruptions (needs a free API key).
- **Each trip day:** park hours, Extra Magic Hours, weather, sunset and every show time.

## ★ Mine tab
- **Must-do list** with live status and progress.
- **Best times to ride:** an hour-by-hour strip per ride from our history, with the quietest hours and each park's quietest times.
- **🔔 Notifications** for this phone, each type switchable.
- **🩺 Notification check:** tests every step from phone to server.
- **Ride alerts** set on this phone.
- **🎨 Theme:** Auto / Light / Dark / Black (OLED) modes and 8 accent colours that tint the whole app, saved per phone.
- **🗓 Visits:** keep every trip to the resort. Add the next one, and look back at any past visit's rides, shows, Wrapped, badges and map.
- **✨ Trip Wrapped:** rides, shows, food, shops, characters, time queued, records and a party leaderboard, plus a full-screen story with a rider type and awards for everyone, which opens first once the trip has ended.
- **🏅 Badges:** 39 badges with images you can save or share.
- **🔥 Roast badges:** cheeky badges with punchlines that end the story, with an opt-out on Mine.
- **🎭 I watched it:** log the shows you saw, listed on Mine and included in your Wrapped.
- **🍽 I ate here / 🛍 I shopped here / 👀 I perused here:** log restaurants and shops from their cards, listed on Mine.
- **💶 Spending:** add an amount when you log a meal or shop, then see totals by type, day and person, and an optional party budget for each visit.
- **🤝 Character collection:** a checklist of characters you've met or seen, with progress, optional photos, your own additions and 6 badges for meetings.
- **📊 Compare visits:** two visits side by side, with top rides, what's new and what you skipped.
- **🗺️ Map history:** everyone's trip as numbered stops on the map, with a replay.
- **📸 Wrapped image:** your Wrapped as a picture for Stories or feed posts.
- **Ride log** by day (who rode, wait, queue type, notes), with Everyone / Just me.
- **JSON export** of your data.

## 🎯 What now? (header button)
- **Your best 3 options right now,** based on where you are:
  - **Rides** ranked by wait plus walk, with "unusually quiet", single-rider savings and last chances.
  - **Shows** you should leave for soon.
  - **Bookings** coming up.
- **Nearest shortcuts:** toilet, baby change, water, first aid, cash, rain shelter, seat, smoking area.

## 📖 Guide (header button)
- **🏨 Hotels:**
  - **Room finder** for the 7 Disney hotels, plus partner hotels.
  - **Hotel benefits** and an Extra Magic Hours guide.
  - **Room-request builder** that writes an English + French message.
- **🍽 Dining:**
  - **Reservation finder** with booking windows and links.
  - **Menus,** party ratings, and a meal-plan and voucher guide.
- **⚡ Skip line:**
  - **"Is Premier Access worth it?"** per ride, with an **Ultimate break-even** calculator.
  - **Live single-rider savings,** plus a virtual-queue guide (e.g. Anna & Elsa).
- **🎢 Park info:**
  - **Attraction directory** by land with a child-height filter.
  - **Refurbishments** (live and scheduled), today's parades and character meets.
  - **Rider Switch.**
- **♿ Accessibility:** Priority Card and Easy Access Card.
- **🚆 Transport:** Eurostar, RER A, Magical Shuttle.
- **🎉 Seasonal events** with dates.
- **Sources** listed for every section.

## 🔔 Notifications
These use standard Web Push from our own server, so they work with the app closed. Ride alerts, plan warnings, show reminders and last chance only go out on trip days.
- **Ride alerts:** a ride reopens, its wait drops to your target, or it goes down.
- **Show reminders** for ★ shows.
- **Booking reminders** 30 minutes before.
- **☀️ Morning brief** on trip days: hours, weather, trains and bookings.
- **⏳ Last chance** about 45 minutes before closing, for unridden must-dos.
- **Plan warnings** when a ride in your plan stops.
- **In-app fallbacks** for alerts, leave alerts and last chance.

## 👥 Party & accounts
- **Sign-in:** single sign-on (OIDC, e.g. Casdoor) or a shared PIN, with lockouts against guessing.
- **PIN profiles:** after the PIN, choose or create your profile (name plus an emoji avatar). Switch any time from the header or Mine. Each profile has its own ride log ("Just me"), last-chance alerts, map name and push notifications.
- **🎨 Profile pictures:** everyone, single sign-on or PIN, can pick an emoji (or initials) or upload a photo (cropped and shrunk on the phone). Shown in the header, on Mine and in the profile list.
- **Header pill** shows who's signed in.
- **Shared with the party:** must-dos, bookings, ratings, ride log (who rode what), locations and the meet-up pin.

## ℹ️ About, Features & Docs
- **Footer on every screen:** when cron last ran, **ℹ️ About · Features · Docs**, and checkers for the site's security headers and DNSSEC.
- **About:** what the app is, where the data comes from, privacy, install instructions, and status.
- **Features:** this list. **Docs:** a full user guide and organiser's guide, with a jump-to contents list and optional screenshots.
- **Both pages are read from the server** (`FEATURES.md`, `DOCS.md`), so editing the files updates the app. The trip dates and app name are filled in from the config.

## 📲 Install as an app
- **Android:** Chrome ⋮ → **Install app** (or the **📲 Install Park Pulse** button under ℹ️ About).
- **iPhone/iPad:** Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen** (needed for notifications).
- Full step-by-step instructions are under **ℹ️ About** in the app.

## ⚙️ Behind the scenes
- **Cron collector:**
  - **Saves wait snapshots** and status changes every 5 minutes, which power history, best times, downtime and the crowd meter.
  - **Sends notifications** and keeps the caches warm.
  - **Skips work at night** and never runs twice at once.
- **Footer** shows when cron last ran.
- **`/api/health` checks** every dependency: PHP, the database, outgoing connections, data sources, push, cron and sign-in.
- **Works offline:** a service worker, the trip pack and saved state on the phone.
- **Security:**
  - **Headers:** CSP, COEP, COOP, CORP and HSTS.
  - **A CSRF guard** on every write.
  - **Editable content files** blocked from the web.
  - **Footer links** to the security-headers and DNSSEC checkers.
- **iPhone layout:** a fixed header that clears the status-bar blur, and safe-area handling.
- **Editable content files** (changeable over FTP): characters, Disney Village, opening hours, ride facts, crowd calendar, touring plans and guide.
